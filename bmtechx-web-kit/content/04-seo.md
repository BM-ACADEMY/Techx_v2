## BLOCK 8 — FAQ

**How long does SEO take to show results in India?**
Three to six months for meaningful movement; longer on competitive terms like "digital marketing agency in Pondicherry". Local map results can move faster — sometimes four to six weeks — because Google Business Profile responds more quickly than organic rankings. [The full answer is here.](/blog/how-long-does-seo-take-india/)

**Can you guarantee first position on Google?**
No, and neither can anyone else. Rankings depend on competition, your website's quality, your location relative to the searcher, and Google's algorithms — several of which are outside anyone's control. What we can commit to is the work: a specific number of optimised pages, articles, citations and reports every month, listed above.

**What's the difference between SEO and Google Ads?**
Ads put you at the top today and stop the moment you stop paying. SEO builds slowly and keeps working. Most businesses under ₹50,000 a month in marketing budget should do one properly rather than both badly. [We compare them with real rupee figures here.](/blog/seo-vs-google-ads-india/)

**Do I need a website for SEO to work?**
For organic search, yes — Google needs something to rank. For local map results, a well-run Google Business Profile does a surprising amount on its own, which is why it's in every plan including Starter.

**Why is there a three-month minimum?**
Because one month of SEO produces nothing worth showing you, and we'd rather not take money for that. Three months is the shortest honest engagement.

**Do you work with businesses outside Pondicherry?**
Yes — Cuddalore, Villupuram, Chennai, Madurai, Trichy and Namakkal. SEO is delivered entirely remotely.

**Will you work with my competitor?**
Not in the same city for the same service. Tell us your main competitors at the start and we'll confirm.
