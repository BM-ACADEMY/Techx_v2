## BLOCK 3 — WHY PROPERTY LEADS GO WRONG

Most developers we meet have already run Facebook ads and been disappointed. It's usually one of these:

**1. The leads were never called.** A property enquiry goes cold in hours. If your telecaller reaches them the next afternoon, most of what you paid for is already gone. This is the single biggest cause of "Facebook ads don't work for property".

**2. Nobody filtered the numbers.** Lead forms collect a lot of wrong numbers and idle curiosity. We phone-verify before handover so your team calls people who actually answered.

**3. The ad promised the wrong thing.** "Plots available" gets clicks from everyone. "DTCP-approved plots from ₹7.5 lakh on NH-44, bank loan available" gets fewer clicks and far more site visits.

**4. No follow-up after the first call.** Property decisions take weeks. One call and silence loses the deal to whoever called three times.

On recent residential plot campaigns across Madurai, Trichy and Namakkal we worked to a target of under ₹30–40 per verified lead. It varies by district, price band and how good the offer is. Villas and premium property cost more per lead and convert at higher value.

We work to an agreed lead volume at an agreed target cost per lead, and we report against both. What we can't guarantee is how many buy — that depends on your price, your site and your sales team.
