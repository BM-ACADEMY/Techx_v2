> This is the single strongest differentiator on the page. Give it room.

Selling online usually means a monthly bill that never ends — a platform subscription, a theme licence, an app for shipping, another app for discounts. Over three years that quietly becomes more than the store cost to build.

**Ours is ₹9,999 once. There is no yearly renewal fee.**

## BLOCK 6 — FAQ

**Is ₹9,999 really the full price?**
For the store as described above, yes, plus 18% GST. The costs we don't charge — payment gateway percentage, courier, ad spend — are listed in the table above so you can budget honestly.

**Really no yearly fee?**
Correct. The ₹9,999 online store has no yearly renewal. Our ₹2,999 and ₹4,999 websites do renew at ₹1,999 a year for domain and server — the store does not.

**Which payment gateway do you use?**
Razorpay, PayU or Cashfree — whichever approves your business type fastest. The account is in your name and the money goes directly to your bank. We never sit between you and your revenue.

**How many products can I add?**
Unlimited. We load your first 20 as part of the build; after that you add them yourself through the dashboard, and we'll show you how in a 20-minute call.

**Can I sell in Tamil Nadu only, or all India?**
Anywhere you can ship. You set delivery areas and charges yourself.

**What about GST on my online sales?**
That's between you and your accountant, and it depends on turnover and what you sell. We build the store to record what you need; we're not tax advisors and we won't pretend to be.
