**How much does a website cost in Pondicherry?**
A single-page website costs ₹2,999 plus GST and goes live in 48 working hours, with the domain and server included for the first year. A multi-page site is ₹4,999 plus GST. An online store with unlimited products is ₹9,999 plus GST. These are fixed prices, not starting prices.

**Do I have to pay every year?**
The ₹2,999 and ₹4,999 websites renew at ₹1,999 plus GST a year, which covers your domain and server. The ₹9,999 online store has no yearly renewal fee at all.

**Is GST included in your prices?**
No. All prices shown exclude 18% GST. So a ₹2,999 website is ₹3,539 including GST. We show the pre-GST figure because that is how the invoice reads.

**How long does SEO take to show results?**
Three to six months for meaningful movement, and longer on competitive terms. Anyone promising you first position in 30 days is telling you something that is not true. Our minimum engagement is three months because anything shorter cannot produce a result worth showing you.

**Do you work outside Pondicherry?**
Yes. We work across Cuddalore, Villupuram, Chennai, Madurai, Trichy and Namakkal. Website, SEO, ads and social work are all delivered remotely. Only photo and video shoots need us on site, and shoots outside Puducherry are priced on requirement.

**What happens if you miss the delivery date?**
The 48-working-hour clock starts when we have your content and your logo, and you can see it running. If we miss it for a reason that is ours, you do not pay the balance until it is live. We would rather commit to a date and be held to it than give you a vague estimate.
