## BLOCK 3 — WHAT WE REPORT, AND WHAT WE DON'T

> Trigger 7. This block is the reason to choose you over the cheaper freelancer.

Ours opens with: **how many people messaged you this month, and where they came from.** Then follower growth, saves and shares — the ones that actually signal interest — and last, reach.

If a month was bad we'll say so, tell you why, and tell you what we're changing. You'll find out from us before you find out from your own bank balance.

## BLOCK 4 — WHO THIS IS NOT FOR

- **If you need enquiries in the next 30 days** — social media builds an audience over months. Ads are faster and we'll say so.
- **If you can't supply photos or let us shoot** — good social needs real material from your business. We can't build a month of content from a logo.
- **If you want follower-count growth as the goal** — we can't optimise for that honestly. Bought followers are visible and they damage the account.
- **If nobody on your side can approve content within two days** — approvals are where these engagements stall, and then the calendar slips and it looks like our fault.

## BLOCK 5 — FAQ

**How much does social media management cost in India?**
Ours starts at ₹6,999 a month plus GST for one platform. What varies most between agencies is how much is actually produced — always ask how many posts, how many reels, and who replies to the messages.

**Do you reply to comments and DMs?**
Yes, during business hours on all plans. For after-hours replies our [WhatsApp automation](/services/whatsapp-automation/) handles the first response and hands over to a person in the morning.

**Tamil or English?**
Mostly English with occasional Tamil, which is what performs best across Tamil Nadu and Puducherry for business audiences. If your customers are Tamil-first we'll shift the mix — we just won't do it by default.

**Do you handle the shoot?**
Yes, quoted separately. Mobile shoots and camera shoots are priced differently; outside Puducherry is priced on requirement.

**Can I see the content before it goes out?**
Yes. The calendar is shared in advance on every plan.
