## BLOCK 3 — THE 48-HOUR CLOCK, EXPLAINED

People ask how 48 hours is possible. Here's the honest answer.

The clock starts when we have **three things**: your logo, your text, and your photos. Not before. Most delays in this industry are not the agency being slow — they're waiting on content from the client, then blaming the calendar.

So we do it in this order:

1. **Hour 0** — you send logo, text, photos on WhatsApp. We confirm the clock has started.
2. **Hour 0–4** — structure and layout agreed. You see a preview link.
3. **Hour 4–36** — we build.
4. **Hour 36–44** — you review, we make changes.
5. **Hour 44–48** — live, on your domain, in your name.

If you don't have the text ready, say so — we'll tell you exactly what to write, or quote separately for writing it. What we won't do is start the clock and then let it run while we wait for you.

## BLOCK 4 — WHY CUSTOM-CODED

Most agencies in this market install WordPress, buy a ₹3,000 theme, and hand it over. That works, and for some businesses it's genuinely the right answer — we say so in [our honest comparison](/blog/wordpress-vs-custom-coded/).

Here's what you get with custom code instead:

- **It loads faster.** No plugin stack, no theme bloat. That matters for Google rankings and it matters for a customer on a 4G connection in Cuddalore.
- **It doesn't break on its own.** A WordPress site with twelve plugins breaks when one of them updates. Yours has nothing to update.
- **Nobody can hack the plugin you forgot about.** Most small-business site hacks come through an out-of-date plugin.
- **You're not renting.** No premium theme licence, no page-builder subscription, no "the developer who built it has the login".

**Test us on this during the call:** send us your current website address and we'll run its loading speed while you watch. Then we'll run ours.
