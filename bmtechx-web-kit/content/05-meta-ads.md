## BLOCK 2 — HOW THE MONEY WORKS

> Trigger 1. Ad-spend confusion is where most agency relationships in this market go bad.

There are two separate amounts and we keep them completely separate:

**1. Our management fee — ₹4,999/month + GST.** That's our work: campaign setup, creative direction, audience testing, daily monitoring, lead delivery, reporting.

**2. Your ad spend — paid by you, directly to Meta.** It goes on your card, in your ad account, in your name. We never take your ad budget into our account and we never mark it up.

Why it matters: if an agency bundles ad spend into one figure, you can't see how much actually reached Meta. Ours is always two numbers, and you can log into your own ad account any time and check.

**How much ad spend do you need?** Realistically ₹10,000–₹15,000 a month to learn anything useful, and more for competitive sectors. Below about ₹300 a day, Meta doesn't get enough data to optimise and you're mostly buying noise. We'll say so if your budget is too thin rather than take the fee anyway.

## BLOCK 5 — WHY ADS STOP WORKING

Most businesses that tried Facebook ads and gave up hit one of four things:

1. **Boosted a post instead of running a campaign.** Boosting optimises for engagement — likes from people who will never buy. A lead campaign optimises for enquiries. Different button, completely different outcome.
2. **Changed the budget every two days.** Every change restarts Meta's learning. Patience is a setting.
3. **No pixel, or a broken one.** Without conversion data flowing back, Meta is guessing about who to show your ad to.
4. **Leads came in and nobody called them.** The most common failure by a distance, and the one that has nothing to do with the ads.

If number four sounds familiar, our [WhatsApp automation](/services/whatsapp-automation/) is the fix, not more ad spend.

Our fee is ₹4,999 a month plus GST. Ad spend is separate, paid by you to Meta, typically ₹10,000–₹15,000 a month minimum to be effective. Agencies charging a percentage of ad spend have an incentive to spend more of your money; we charge a flat fee for that reason.

**Whose ad account is it?**
Yours. We request access to it. If we part ways you keep the account, the pixel, the audiences and all the campaign history.

**How fast do leads arrive?**
Within hours of a campaign going live, usually. Whether they're good leads depends on targeting and creative, which is what the first two weeks are for.

**Do you make the videos and images?**
We direct them and we produce them — shoots are priced separately, with mobile shoots and camera shoots quoted differently, and shoots outside Puducherry priced on requirement.

**Should I do ads or SEO first?**
If you need enquiries this month, ads. If you're building something for the next three years, SEO. With a limited budget, one done properly beats both done badly. [Full comparison with rupee figures.](/blog/seo-vs-google-ads-india/)
